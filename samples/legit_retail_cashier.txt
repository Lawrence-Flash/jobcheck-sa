Cashier

Mthimkhulu Grocer (Pty) Ltd
Shop 4, 88 Commissioner Street, Johannesburg, 2001

Full-time cashier for a neighbourhood grocery store.

Experience on a till is preferred. New staff are trained on the point-of-sale system.

Pay rate: R28.50 per hour, about R5 500 per month.
Hours: Tuesday to Sunday. The weekly roster is published each Monday.

Apply in person at the store, or email a CV to jobs@mthimkhulugrocer.co.za.
Shortlisted people are interviewed at the store. Bring your ID to the interview.
